CrabGrab — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 19, 2026

CrabGrab does not collect, store or transmit any personal or browsing data. Everything happens on your device.

What data is stored

  • "Find full size" preference: a single value (on/off) saved through Chrome sync to remember your setting across your own devices. It is never sent to any server of ours.
  • Temporary per-tab image list: the addresses of the images a page loads are kept in session memory only, to show the "Network images" gallery. They are cleared automatically when you close the tab or the browser.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not collect personally identifiable information.
  • We do not log your browsing history.
  • We do not use cookies, analytics or third-party services.
  • We do not sell or share data with anyone.

Permissions

The permissions the extension requests are used solely to download images: saving the file, adding the right-click option, temporarily adjusting the Referer header to avoid 403 errors, and listing the images the page loaded. They are not used to collect data.

Changes to this policy

If these practices change in the future, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date.
